F1: Pseudoreplication — cells treated as independent biological replicates in limma [BLIND+ENRICHED]

  • Location: discordanceLimma.R:55-66, 129-203; Figure3i_v2.R:29-43; GBM_Combinations_ISOSCELES_2020L1000.R:192-203; GBM_scFOCAL_inVitro_Combinations.R:192-203; CT179_RNAseq/ct179_combinationScoring.R:54-75; Shiny inst/shiny/server.R:1961-1975, 2018-2032
  • What's wrong: model.matrix(~0 + patient_group) treats every cell as independent → residual DOF ≈ N_cells (10⁴+) instead of N_patients (11). Yields B>6500, P=0 underflow, t≈121 with logFC=0.007.
  • Evidence: supp_dataset5_patient_drugdiscordancelimma_re.csv — 15,007/18,414 (81.5%) reach adj.p<0.05; 205 rows have P.Value exactly 0; AveExpr essentially constant at 9.806±0.014 (the enrichment-flagged "triad").
  • Verified: ✓ all numerics independently reproduced.
  • Data-impact: MAJOR (structural — inflation factor √(N_cells/2) ≈ 50× on t; "N at adj.p<0.05" claims invalid)
  • Paper-impact: MAJOR for significance claims; MODERATE for top-K rank-based selection (rank-order preserved because all compounds inflate equally — alisertib still ranks 1/1674 by mean t across all 11 patient contrasts)
  • Novelty: NOVEL / CLASS-KNOWN (Squair 2021, Crowell 2020 are canonical for scRNA-seq pseudoreplication; no scFOCAL-specific report)
  • Enrichment contribution: wrong_value_findings.json line 198 named the AveExpr+logFC+B triad directly — saved 30+ min of pipeline tracing
  • Fix: pseudobulk per (patient × condition), OR duplicateCorrelation(block=patientID), OR mixed-effects via muscat/dream. Note: library(lme4) is loaded but never used (F24) — fossil of the intended-but-abandoned correct analysis.

Headline Impact on the Paper

What still stands (deposited data matches paper exactly, claims survive): - Combo > ABT-414 survival benefit (Fig 5E MS=63 vs 38 vs 24 vs 19, p=0.0014 combo vs ABT-414, reproduces exactly). - Alisertib ranks #1 by mean t across all 11 patient discordance contrasts (rank-based selection robust to F1's √N inflation). - Aurora kinase signature in top 10 (top 5: alisertib, NVP.TAE684, GSK.1070916, MK.1775, PF.03758309). - Fig 3i direction (alisertib reduces NPC-like, increases MES-like) survives qualitatively even after F7 structural correction.

What is overstated by the deposited data: - 81% of compounds adj.p<0.05 in ds5p: pseudoreplication-driven (F1 + F3 combine). - Fig 3i FDR=3.2e-238: structural N-inflation from one-sample-vs-0 with N=10,394 (F7). - Fig 5D "Adj.P < 0.000001" at days 25/28/32: degenerate two-sample t with Vehicle N=1, SD=0 (F9a); valid-day max is t=3.13 at day 17. - "ρ=0.7 BLISS validation": aggregate over 18/24 individually-NS cell lines + 3 anti-correlated (F6). - "varlitinib top EGFR hit": ranks 31/411 by paper's own CI metric (F4). - "OLIG2 inhibitor as therapy": monotherapy NS vs vehicle; combo benefit mostly ABT-414 (F17). - "54 of 64 oncology drugs significant": cannot be reproduced; max sig per contrast = 47 (F13).

What is unverifiable due to missing inputs (paper-impact must be argued structurally): - DMSO arm of in-vivo alisertib experiment (F7) — 5,708 cells stripped. - CT-179 GBM8 raw RNA-seq counts (F8) — 841-gene signature derivation black-boxed. - Per-cell × per-compound connectivity matrices — blocks rerun of F1/F3/F12. - Patient scRNA-seq raw counts (Synapse-gated) — blocks F22 75th-percentile cutoff audit.

Key Takeaways

  • Data authenticity: PARTIAL. The deposited supp tables are a curated, partially-Excel-mediated subset of the analysis substrate. Where the deposit IS the substrate (survival, alisertib subset, BLISS heatmap, per-patient rankings), reproductions match cleanly. Where it is a derived artifact (Fig 2h drug counts, CT-179 signature, in-vivo DMSO arm, landmark count), paper claims cannot be adjudicated and impact must be argued structurally.
  • 32 of 33 findings VERIFIED on independent re-derivation (F5 partially-verified with 0-index nit, substance unchanged; F32 is documented as not-a-bug).
  • All 33 findings are NOVEL for this paper — no PubPeer thread, no GitHub issue, no Matters Arising. Six classes (F1, F2, F4, F7, F11, F13) are publishable as a Matters Arising or PubPeer thread without scoop risk.
  • Top finding by paper-impact: F1 + F3 (pseudoreplication + circular dichotomization) — together invalidate the limma significance layer of the ISOSCELES discordance pipeline. Mitigated by the fact that rank-order survives, so qualitative discoveries (alisertib for NPC-like cells, OLIG2-inhibitor + EGFR-ADC combo) likely stand even with corrected statistics.
  • F7 (DMSO missing) is the most surprising structural gap — Fig 3i/j's spectacular FDRs (3.2e-238) are a one-sample-vs-0 test framed as a two-sample alisertib-vs-DMSO contrast, and re-analysis is structurally blocked because DMSO cells are absent from the deposit.
  • F9 + F9a together break Fig 5D: imaging dropout selects slower-growing tumors (downward-bias Vehicle mean), then degenerate t-tests at N=1 / SD=0 produce eye-popping but undefined p-values. Survival (Fig 5E) is the load-bearing evidence and is unaffected.
  • Caveat: PubPeer search-engine indexing is patchy; manual browser visit to pubpeer.com/search?q=10.1038%2Fs41467-025-67783-5 recommended before any public critique.
